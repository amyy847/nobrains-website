NOBRAINS enquiry form: setup (about 10 minutes)

Do all of this while signed in to nobrainscollective@gmail.com, so the emails go out from that address.

1. Go to sheets.google.com and create a blank spreadsheet. Name it "NOBRAINS enquiries".
2. In that sheet: Extensions > Apps Script.
3. Delete what is in the editor, paste in everything from Code.gs, and click the save icon.
4. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
     Execute as: Me (nobrainscollective@gmail.com)
     Who has access: Anyone
   Click Deploy.
5. Google asks for permission. Click "Authorize access", pick the account, then "Advanced" >
   "Go to (project name) (unsafe)" > Allow. (It says unsafe only because it is your own unpublished script.)
6. Copy the "Web app" address (it ends in /exec) and send it to whoever maintains the website.
   That address is pasted into FORM_ENDPOINT in assets/js/main.js.
7. To test: open the address in a browser. You should see {"ok":true}.

Every enquiry then:
  - becomes a new row in the "Enquiries" tab of the sheet,
  - arrives in the inbox as "New enquiry from <name>" (press Reply to answer the visitor),
  - sends the visitor an automatic "We got your message" email from nobrainscollective@gmail.com.

To get an Excel file: in the sheet, File > Download > Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).

If the script is ever edited: Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: New version > Deploy.
(The web address stays the same.)

Limits: a free Gmail account can send about 100 emails a day through scripts, and each enquiry uses 2.
Automatic emails sent from a Gmail address occasionally land in the visitor's spam folder.
